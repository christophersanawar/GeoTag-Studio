Why Geo Tags Disappear on WhatsApp (and How to Keep Them)

Quick answer: WhatsApp, many email services and most social apps remove EXIF metadata, including GPS geo tags, to protect privacy and reduce file size. To keep your geotags, move photos by USB, cloud download or direct file transfer, then upload them straight to Google Business Profile.

You carefully tag a batch of photos, send them to a teammate on WhatsApp, and the location is gone. This is normal behaviour, not a bug in your tool.

Why apps remove metadata

Photos can reveal where someone lives or works. To protect users, messaging and social platforms strip EXIF data when you share. They also recompress the image, which reduces file size.

How to move photos safely

  • USB cable or card reader from phone to computer.
  • Cloud storage such as Google Drive: upload the ZIP and download the original files.
  • Send as document: some apps keep more data when files are sent as documents, but it is not guaranteed, so verify.
  • Upload directly from the device that holds the tagged files.

How to check that tags survived

On Windows, right-click the photo, open Properties and look at the Details tab for GPS and Keywords. On Mac, open the photo in Preview and use Tools, then Show Inspector. On a phone, open the photo info panel.

Other ways metadata is lost

  • Screenshots create new files without the original data.
  • Photo editors and compressors may remove EXIF when exporting.
  • HEIC photos may fail to process; use JPEG.

Best workflow for teams

Let one person tag and upload. If different people must hand over photos, share the ZIP through cloud storage. Learn the full process in our step-by-step geotagging guide.
